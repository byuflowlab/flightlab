FLIGHTLAB FOR WINDOWS

1. Double-click "Start FlightLab.cmd".
2. Keep the launcher window open while you use FlightLab in your browser.
3. Close the launcher window when you are finished.

The first launch needs internet access and may take several minutes. Later
launches are faster. FlightLab checks for course updates automatically.

If Windows SmartScreen appears, choose More info, verify that the file came
from the ME 415 course, and choose Run anyway.

If setup fails, take a screenshot of the launcher window and send it to your TA.
